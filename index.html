How to Play

Dodge falling bombs and their blasts for 5 one-minute waves. When the final timer ends, survive the remaining blasts. Catch airdrops for extra lives.

Each wave drops one giant bomb at a random moment — twice as big, faster, and it costs two lives. Level 5 drops a second randomized giant bomb.

Soldiers enter from both sides, walk toward you, and shoot. Jump to dodge bullets. A bomb blast kills them. Contact or a bullet costs a life.

Level 4 adds a third soldier from a random side. Level 5 sends two synchronized pairs, plus one extra soldier from a random side at a random time.

Pulsing rings mark each bomb's blast zone. Giant bombs use a wider double ring. The ring grows brighter as impact approaches. Tiny strikes against the distant city are atmosphere only.

Keyboard: ← → or A D to move; Space W or ↑ to jump.
Mouse: move the pointer to steer; click the playfield or JUMP to jump.
Touch: drag to steer; swipe up or tap JUMP to jump.

Hold the jump key, mouse button, or JUMP for a slightly higher, longer jump over soldiers. Move toward the soldier as you jump. On touch, you can also swipe up and keep your finger down, or steer with one finger while holding JUMP with another.

Click or tap menu buttons. With a keyboard, use Tab to select a button and Enter or Space to activate it.

Esc or P pauses. YouTube mute controls the sound. FX: Auto follows your device's reduced-motion preference; Full and Reduced override it.
